What it is

LakoOS is a free operating system on the NT architecture with a familiar desktop, maintained by LakoMoor.

Credits

LakoOS builds on the work of many open source contributors. They are credited in CREDITS.md, and their copyright notices stay in the source files.

License

GNU GPL 3.0. Inherited code keeps the license stated in its file headers, mostly GPL 2.0 or later. The full texts are in the repository.
